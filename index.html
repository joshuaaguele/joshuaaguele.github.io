Joshua Osejigbe Aguele ↗

I bring statistics and data science to questions about places, people and work.

I hold a BSc in Statistics from Ambrose Alli University, Nigeria. My independent projects include environmental analysis across Dutch municipalities, comparisons of online search interest with official labour-market data, and data visualisation.

My reports and preprints are linked above so you can assess the work directly. This page will grow as I complete and share more projects.
