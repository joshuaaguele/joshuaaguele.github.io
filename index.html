02 / ABOUT ME

Statistics.
With a human context.

My interest in health data began with statistical questions—and grew through hospital work.

I hold a BSc in Statistics from Ambrose Alli University, Nigeria. My undergraduate thesis used tuberculosis data to compare CUSUM and EWMA methods for early detection. That work introduced me to the challenge of recognising meaningful changes in health data.

At Berta Medical Centre, I worked with patient records, analysed hospital data and prepared reporting for the hospital’s National Health Insurance Scheme (NHIS) department. I also recommended medical software, migrated records and trained doctors and nurses. During an earlier internship, I coded a BMI calculator in MATLAB that doctors used.

These experiences shape my growing focus on biostatistics and health data science: combining statistical reasoning, programming and an understanding of how clinical data are recorded. I am interested in developing this further through survival analysis, longitudinal data and carefully evaluated prediction models.

My interests also extend beyond healthcare. My independent work includes environmental and spatial analysis across Dutch municipalities, Google Trends and CBS labour-market research, and a SQL and Power BI dashboard. The reports, preprints and repositories let you explore that work directly.
